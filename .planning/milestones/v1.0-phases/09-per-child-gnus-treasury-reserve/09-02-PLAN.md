## Note on Double-Spend of Earmarked Minions

The orchestrator's threat-model brief lists "double-spend of earmarked minions" as a concern. **This threat is mooted by the conversion-native model (D1)**: there are no earmarked minions and no reserve ledger. The escrow function is served by supply itself — a holder can only convert what they hold (enforced by `_burn`'s balance check). CONCERNS #1 dies by construction, not by escrow accounting.
</threat_model>
